Rules the Brain Follows
R1
Regular Buyer Detection
Detects customers buying on alternating days or specific weekdays. Reserves stock for their pattern.
R2
Weekend Spike Detection
Identifies products with higher Fri/Sat/Sun sales. Calculates weekend uplift % and orders extra.
R3
Frequency Classification
Categorises every product: daily seller (80%+ days), regular (40%+), occasional (20%+), or rare.
R4
Trending Detection
Compares first-half vs second-half sales. Flags products trending up or down by 30%+.
R5
Order Calculation
order = predicted_weekly_demand − current_stock + 10% safety buffer. Never negative.
R6
Priority Scoring
Critical if stock covers <2 days. Important if stock < 1 week demand. Normal otherwise.
